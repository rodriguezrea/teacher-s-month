Dear Ma'am Marifel and Sir Jayboy,

Happy Teacher's Month! 🌷

We would like to take this opportunity to say thank you for all the time, effort, patience, and understanding that you give to your students. We truly appreciate everything you do for us, both inside and outside the classroom.

Thank you for sharing your knowledge with us and for continuing to guide us even when things become challenging. Your lessons are not only something we learn for school, but also lessons that can help us as we continue to grow and reach our dreams.

We may not always say it, and sometimes we may not realize how much effort you put into teaching us, but please know that your hard work is noticed and appreciated.

Thank you for inspiring us to keep learning, keep trying, and believe in ourselves. ♡

Having teachers who are willing to understand, guide, and support their students is something we are truly grateful for. The things you teach us and the memories we make with you are things that we will remember even as we move forward.

We hope that this simple message reminds you that your efforts matter and that your students are thankful to have you as our teachers.

Happy Teacher's Month, Ma'am Marifel and Sir Jayboy! 💐

Thank you for being wonderful teachers and for making a difference in our lives.
